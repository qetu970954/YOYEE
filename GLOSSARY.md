# YOYEE learning materials

Terms distinguish the original course structure from the site's reading units.

## Language

**Source chapter**:
A topic block defined by a chapter divider in an original Bootlin slide deck. It may occupy several reading chunks or share a chunk with adjacent chapters.
_Avoid_: Reading chunk, when referring to the original chapter boundaries

**Reading chunk**:
A contiguous, coherent group of slides intended for roughly 25 minutes of general reading. This is the learning unit presented as a section on the site.
_Avoid_: Source chapter, when referring to the site's balanced reading units

**Estimated reading time**:
An approximate duration for reading and understanding a chunk's text, diagrams, code and worked examples, excluding hands-on exercises, tool installation and build waits. It is not personalized or a guarantee of actual study duration.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed duration, lab execution time
